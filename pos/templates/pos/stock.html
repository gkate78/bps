Record a restock

What you bought, in the material's unit (a ream of 500 sheets = 500), and what it cost in total. The cost per unit becomes the average of what's on hand and what you bought.

Record a count

What's actually on the shelf. BPS records the difference from what it expected (spoiled sheets, misprints, use not rung up) and uses your count from now on.

What each item uses

Per copy or piece sold (quantity 1); a fraction works too (an ID picture package is 1/3 of an A4 photo paper, a wallet-size photo 1/2 of a 3R). Items come from Settings → Service items; a sale only takes stock when its description is one of them. Back to back still uses 1 sheet.
